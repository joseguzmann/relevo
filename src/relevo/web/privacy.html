Relevo privacy policy

Relevo is a personal tool. It has one user, its owner, and is not offered to anyone else.

Google data

Relevo asks for one Gmail permission: sending email. It sends a message only after the owner approves that specific message in the app. It does not read, label or delete email.

Storage and sharing

The access token and the messages sent are kept on the owner's private server. They are not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising or to train models.

Removal

The owner can revoke access at any time from their Google account, and deleting the server data removes the rest.
